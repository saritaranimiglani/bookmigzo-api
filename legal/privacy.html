Migzo Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

This policy covers the Migzo app (Android package com.bookmigzo.app, iOS app ID 6799587029). Migzo is operated by Sarita Rani Miglani, a sole proprietor in India ("we", "us").

Migzo has closed

Migzo stopped operating on 20 November 2026 and its servers have been shut down. The app is kept on the app stores as a showcase. It runs offline with sample restaurants and menus built into the app. You cannot create an account, sign in, place an order or make a payment.

What the app collects now

Nothing that reaches us. The app does not send your personal information to Migzo, and it has no advertising or analytics.

  • Location (optional). If you allow it, the app reads your location on your device to suggest the nearest sample city. It is not sent to us. Your phone's own maps service may be used to name your city.
  • Anything you type (for example a phone number on the sign-in screen) stays on your device and goes nowhere, because sign-in is turned off.
  • App updates. The app checks Expo, our app-update provider, for updates. That check carries technical details such as the platform, app version and an anonymous install identifier, handled by Expo under its own privacy policy.
  • Notifications. If you allowed notifications in the past, Apple, Google or Expo may still issue a device token under their own policies. We no longer receive or use it, and the app sends no notifications.

Data from when Migzo was running

Between July and November 2026, Migzo held account details (phone number, and name or email if you gave them), orders, and payment references from our payment provider. Payments were handled by that provider; we never stored customers' card or bank details.

The servers holding this data have been shut down. We keep an offline copy only of the records Indian tax law requires (GST law requires business records to be kept for 72 months after the relevant annual-return due date), and delete it when that period ends. We do not sell or share this data with anyone.

Your choices

You can uninstall the app at any time, and you can turn off location access in your phone's settings. To ask what data we hold about you from when Migzo was running, or to have it deleted (except records we must keep by law), email us. We reply within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 18 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
